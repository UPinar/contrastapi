Corpora
  Each corpus holds the distinct version strings of one ecosystem's OSV advisories (range
  events + listed versions) in a copy of the service's database on the date below. The
  strings come from OSV records. docs/API_Documentation.md § Data sources and licenses
  names each OSV source and its license.
